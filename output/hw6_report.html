No. 1

What caching is worth

The handbook is the same on every call, so the model can reuse it at one-tenth of the price. We ran all 12 practice documents both ways. In the API's own counts, 98% of input came from cache in one setup, and 0 of 24 calls touched the cache in the other.

No. 5  ·  For the Store Manager

Our recommendation

Yes, turn it on for real vendor mail, as an advisor to the buyer. It reads quotes quickly and, in our tests, was not fooled once defenses were in place. A person should still make every purchase.

About $9.78 a month

At 1,000 documents a day for 30 days, using the defended agent's average of $0.000326 per document. Rewriting the cache after quiet periods could add up to about $1.76 a month.

  • Keep email and payments off. They stay disconnected until a human signoff is built.
  • Have the buyer approve every order. The agent cannot judge a false claim that is plainly visible.
  • Check the trap log weekly. Any entry means a document got through, so pause and review.
  • Spot-check a sample each month. New tricks will appear, and exam accuracy is not yet confirmed.
